Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

nethack.app is a free, independent fan project. This page says what information the site keeps about you, and what happens to it. For anything about your information, email admin@nethack.app. See also the Terms of Use.

In short

  • Offline games stay in your browser. I never receive them.
  • Online games are kept on the server, and finished ones are public, including every action you take in them and when they were played.
  • No ads, no analytics and no advertising cookies. I don't sell your information.

Offline play

Your offline games, saved games, scores, options, character name, recordings of your games and any tile sets you import are kept in your browser's storage on your device. None of it is sent to me. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it.

If you choose to download a debug package and send it to me, for example by attaching it to an issue on the site's public issue tracker, it contains the recording of your game and details of your browser. Anything you post on the issue tracker is public.

Visiting the site

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your browser's requests and may log details such as your IP address and browser, under its own privacy policy. The site stores things in your browser only to make it work, such as your settings and a copy of the site so it plays offline.

Online play: what's kept

  • Your account: a random account ID, kept by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. If you sign in with Google, it also keeps your Google sign-in details (email and name) private.
  • Your handle, and the requests you make to choose one.
  • Your games: character names, your options and configuration file, every action you take in the game, when the game was played, and each game's result and achievements.
  • The server's record of each game: each request your browser makes to the server, with the time and the server's answer. Google keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Bot check: online play uses Google's bot check (reCAPTCHA). This site is protected by reCAPTCHA, and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

I don't ask for your address, payment details or location. Keep anything private out of your online games, handle and character names.

What it's used for

To run your online games and keep them for you; to show public games, rankings and achievements; to check games are played fairly; to keep the site secure; and to fix problems. By playing online, you agree to it being kept and used for these.

What's public

Finished online games are shared publicly, as on other NetHack servers, under your handle, or as “Anonymous” if you have none. That includes the replay, which is every action you take in the game, when the game was played, the score, how the game ended and achievements. Your account ID and Google sign-in details are never shown.

Who else handles it

  • Google runs the online server (Firebase) and the bot check (reCAPTCHA).
  • Cloudflare hosts the site.
  • I'll share information if the law requires it, or if it's needed to protect the site or its players.
  • If the site changes hands, this policy goes with it.

Google and Cloudflare may keep information in other countries, including the European Union and the United States, under their own data protection terms.

How long it's kept

  • Offline: until you clear it from your browser.
  • Your account, handle and online games: for as long as the site runs, unless you or I delete them.
  • The server's record of each game: for as long as they're needed to check fair play and keep the site secure.

Your choices

  • Play offline, and nothing is sent to me.
  • Email me to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account and games. I may need to check the account is yours first, and I'll answer within 30 days.
  • Deleting removes your games from the site, though others may already have saved a copy of a public game, and your handle may stay reserved.
  • If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

Children

Online play isn't for anyone under 13. If I learn that someone under 13 has played online, I'll delete their account and games.

Security

I take reasonable care of your information, and I'll tell you if it's ever exposed where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date at the top.
